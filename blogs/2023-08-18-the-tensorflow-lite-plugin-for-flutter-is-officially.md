Posted by Paul Ruiz , Developer Relations Engineer We're excited to announce that the TensorFlow Lite plugin for Flutter has been officially migrated to the TensorFlow GitHub account and released! Three years ago, Amish Garg , one of our talented Google Summer of Code contributors, wrote a widely used TensorFlow Lite plugin for Flutter.
